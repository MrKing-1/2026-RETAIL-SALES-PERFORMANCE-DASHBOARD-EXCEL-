## Project Overview

This project presents an interactive Retail Sales Performance Dashboard created using Microsoft Excel
The dashboard analyzes retail sales data to uncover meaningful insights about revenue performance, customer segments, product categories, regional sales trend, pay methods and salesperson performance

## Objectives

- Analyze overall retail sales performance
- Track revenue and customer trends
- Understand payment method preferences
- Evaluation regional sales performance
- Generate actionable business insights and recommendations.

## Key Insights

- Office Equipment leads revenue at $2.6M, outperforming Electronics by $700K.
- North region generates the highest revenue at $1.9M, showing stronger market performance.
- July revenue dropped to $263K, a 53% decline from June’s $564K.
- Corporate customers contribute $3.3M, exceeding Individual customers by $600K.
- Revenue depends heavily on top products and high-performing salespeople.

  ## Recommendations
  
- Increase focus on Office Equipment to maximize the strongest revenue segment.
- Analyse North region strategies and apply them to weaker regions.
- Investigate July decline to identify demand or operational issues.
- Prioritize corporate customer retention through targeted sales strategies.
- Improve product availability and replicate top salesperson practices.
